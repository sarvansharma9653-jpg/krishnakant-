Program Overview (PGP-TL&D)

The Post Graduate Program in Training, Learning & Development (PGP-TL&D) is ISTD's benchmark qualification recognized across India by public and private sector enterprises, government departments, and multinational corporations.

Now enrolling for Batch 95, this comprehensive 15-month program is specifically curated to bridge theory and practice. Whether you are an aspiring corporate trainer, HR specialist, instructional designer, school leader, or university educator, PGP-TL&D equips you with the methodologies to command any learning environment.

Program Delivery Features:

  • Duration: 15 Months (2 Semesters + Internship Project)
  • Class Mode: Online on Sundays with interactive master sessions
  • Recordings: Full access to class video recordings & slide decks
  • Study Material: Digital textbooks, reading dossiers, and case studies included

Key Eligibility

• Graduate or Post Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized University.
• Final year undergraduate / post graduate students awaiting results.
• Working professionals, teachers, corporate executives, and freelance consultants.

Why Recruiters Value PGP-TL&D:

Recognized by the Ministry of HRD (now MoE) and leading corporate employers across India as a gold standard in training management.

Academic Rigor

Course Curriculum (10 Subjects)

Carefully structured into 2 semesters designed by leading academicians and corporate L&D directors.

Semester I: Foundations of Learning & Facilitation
  • Paper 1: Adult Learning Principles & Cognitive Theories
  • Paper 2: Training Needs Assessment (TNA) & Competency Mapping
  • Paper 3: Instructional Design & Curriculum Development
  • Paper 4: Training Methods, Facilitation & Presentation Skills
  • Paper 5: Human Resource Development Systems & Organizational Context
Semester II: Advanced L&D Strategy & Execution
  • Paper 6: Digital Learning, AI in L&D, and Educational Technologies
  • Paper 7: Measurement, Evaluation & ROI of Training (Kirkpatrick Model)
  • Paper 8: Organizational Development, Change Management & Culture
  • Paper 9: Executive Coaching, Mentoring & Counseling Techniques
  • Paper 10: Strategic L&D Leadership, Ethics & Workplace Diversity
Practical Internship Project & Viva Voce

Students undergo a hands-on project in an organization or live educational setting where they conduct end-to-end Training Needs Analysis (TNA), design an intervention, facilitate a session, and evaluate the behavioral outcome. A written project report and viva voce round out the certification.
