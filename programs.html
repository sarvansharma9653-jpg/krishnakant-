Certified Train The Trainer (TTT)

18 - 20 Dec 2026 (Friday - Sunday)

The ISTD Train The Trainer (TTT) program is the definitive national certification for professionals wanting to master facilitation. Participants learn adult learning principles, room management, body language, vocal modulation, interactive games, micro-sessions, and constructive evaluation.

Statutory & Legal Compliance

Certified Trainer's Program on POSH

13 - 15 Nov 2026

Prevention of Sexual Harassment at Workplace (POSH) Act 2013 compliance is legally mandatory for all employers in India. This course certifies you as a recognized POSH trainer, qualified to conduct employee sensitization, train Internal Complaints Committees (ICC), and audit company compliance.
