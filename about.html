National Heritage

The National Society (ISTD)

The Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD), established in April 1970, is a national level professional & non-profit society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

ISTD is dedicated to the promotion and advancement of human resources development through education, training, and development in government, public and private sector enterprises, educational institutions, and society at large.

Affiliated with the International Federation of Training and Development Organizations (IFTDO), Geneva, and the Asian Regional Training and Development Organization (ARTDO), ISTD connects Indian trainers to international standards and best practices.

Regional Presence

ISTD Jaipur Chapter

The Jaipur Chapter has been a pillar of professional development in Rajasthan for decades. Bringing together seasoned HR directors, independent master trainers, university deans, and corporate leaders, the chapter acts as a catalyst for human capital excellence.

Our Vision

To be the premier catalyst in creating world-class training ecosystems and transformative human resource leaders across Rajasthan and India.

Our Mission

To empower learning facilitators with modern pedagogical skills, foster industry-academia collaboration, and uphold high professional ethics.

Core Values

Integrity, continuous learning, inclusivity, innovation in pedagogy, and dedication to organizational and social impact.
